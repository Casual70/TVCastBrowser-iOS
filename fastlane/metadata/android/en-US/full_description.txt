TV Cast Browser is an open-source web browser designed to detect HTML5, HLS (.m3u8), DASH (.mpd), MP4, and WebM video streams on web pages and cast them directly to your Smart TV over local Wi-Fi.

Key features:
• 100% FOSS Cast protocol implementation (mDNS + CASTv2 TLS) with zero proprietary Google Play Services dependencies.
• Built-in Local HTTP Proxy to forward streams requiring custom Referer, Cookie, or CORS headers, including dynamic HLS (#EXTM3U) playlist rewriting.
• Primary Domain Lock: pin your main website and block all external new-tab popups and cross-domain ad redirects.
• Recent history (up to 20 sites).
• Integrated remote control bar (Play, Pause, Stop).
